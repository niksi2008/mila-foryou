This Privacy Policy describes how Mila For You (“we”) handles information when you visit this website, browse the shop, or connect your TikTok account to our publishing tools.

Information we collect

Browsing this catalog does not require an account. If you authorize our TikTok app, we may receive basic profile information (such as open_id, display name, avatar) and permission to list or publish videos you choose to post. We do not sell personal data.

How we use it

We use authorized TikTok data only to publish content you create to your own TikTok account and to operate that account’s public presence. Shop inquiries go through Instagram DMs.

Sharing

We do not share your TikTok tokens with unrelated third parties. Publishing goes through TikTok’s official APIs.

Retention

Access tokens are stored securely and can be revoked at any time from your TikTok account settings.
